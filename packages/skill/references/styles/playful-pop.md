# Playful Pop

Bright saturated colors, chunky rounded shapes, friendly type.

## When to use

- Consumer apps
- Education
- Kid products
- Marketing

## When NOT to use

- Enterprise
- Finance

## Do

- ✅ Saturated palette
- ✅ Rounded everything
- ✅ Bouncy animations

## Don't

- ❌ Use for serious flows
- ❌ Combine with dense data

## Recipe (CSS)

```css
background: var(--vyre-color-primitive-pink-4);
border-radius: var(--vyre-radius-2xl);
padding: var(--vyre-space-6);
font-weight: var(--vyre-font-weight-bold);
```

## Required tokens

- `radius.2xl`
- `palette.candy`

## Performance cost

`low` — no significant cost

## Accessibility

Test contrast carefully with bright pastels.
