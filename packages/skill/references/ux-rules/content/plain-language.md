# Plain Language

> Write at a grade 6-8 reading level for general audiences.

**Severity:** `high`

## Rule

Short sentences. Common words. Active voice. Cut filler. One idea per sentence.

## Do

- 'Save changes' not 'Persist your modifications'
- 'Done' not 'Operation completed successfully'

## Don't

- Jargon in user-facing copy
- 20-word sentences with 4 clauses
