# Honest Truncation

> Long content needs truncation strategy: ellipsis with full content on hover/expand.

**Severity:** `medium`

## Rule

Don't silently truncate critical info. Always provide access to full content.

## Do

- Title with ellipsis + full title on hover
- 'Show more' for body text

## Don't

- Truncate user names with no way to see full name
- Cut off critical numbers (prices, IDs)
