# Title Case vs Sentence Case

> Pick one and stick to it. Sentence case is more modern and easier to read.

**Severity:** `low`

## Rule

Sentence case for body, buttons, labels. Title Case only for proper noun titles or headlines if your brand demands.

## Do

- 'Send invitation' (sentence case)
- Consistent across all UIs

## Don't

- Mix 'Send Invitation' and 'Send invitation' randomly
