# Voice and Tone

> Define a voice (consistent personality) and tone (varies by context).

**Severity:** `medium`

## Rule

Friendly but not flippant in errors. Clear over clever. Match the user's emotional state in the moment.

## Do

- Calm, helpful tone in errors
- Celebratory tone in success
- Direct tone in destructive confirmations

## Don't

- Joke in error messages
- Corporate-speak in friendly UIs
