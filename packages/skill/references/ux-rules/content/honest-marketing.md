# Honest Marketing Copy

> Don't oversell, fake urgency, or use deceptive scarcity claims.

**Severity:** `medium`

## Rule

Real numbers, real stock counts, real testimonials. Fake urgency erodes trust.

## Do

- '12 in stock' when it's true
- Real customer testimonials with attribution

## Don't

- 'Only 3 left!' when there are 30000
- Fake countdown timers
- Misleading 'You saved $X'
