# Disclose AI-Generated Content

> When AI generates user-facing content, label it clearly.

**Severity:** `high`

## Rule

Suggested replies, AI summaries, AI search answers — all need 'AI-generated' or sparkle indicator. Provide source/grounding when possible.

## Do

- Sparkle ✨ icon + 'AI summary'
- Show sources for AI search answers
- Easy way to give feedback / edit

## Don't

- Pass off AI content as human
- Hide AI involvement to seem 'magic'
