# Form Length: Ask for Less

> Every field is friction. Cut anything not strictly necessary.

**Severity:** `high`

## Rule

Audit every field: is it truly needed NOW? Defer non-essential to later. Progressive profiling beats long signup.

## Do

- 3-field signup (email, password, name)
- Defer optional fields to onboarding

## Don't

- 20-field signup forms
- Asking for phone number 'just in case'
