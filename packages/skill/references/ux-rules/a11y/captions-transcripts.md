# Captions and Transcripts

> Pre-recorded video has captions; audio has transcript.

**Severity:** `high`

## Rule

Caption all dialog and meaningful sounds. Provide text transcript for podcasts/audio.

## Do

- Closed captions on all video
- Transcripts for podcasts

## Don't

- Auto-captions only (often wrong)
- Skip captions for 'silent' video that has music
