# No Flashing Content

> No content flashes more than 3 times per second.

**Severity:** `high`

## Rule

Triggers seizures in photosensitive users. Avoid strobes, rapid flashes, sudden bright transitions.

## Do

- Limit pulse animations to <3Hz
- Smooth crossfades instead of cuts

## Don't

- Strobing notification dots
- Flashing 'NEW!' badges
