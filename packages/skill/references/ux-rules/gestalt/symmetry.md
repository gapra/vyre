# Symmetry & Balance

> Symmetric compositions feel stable; asymmetric ones feel dynamic.

**Severity:** `low`

## Rule

Choose symmetry intentionally. Symmetric grids work for catalogs and dashboards; asymmetric layouts work for editorial and marketing.

## Do

- Match column counts for catalog grids
- Use rule-of-thirds for hero compositions

## Don't

- Force symmetry when content lengths differ wildly
- Center everything by default
