# Figure / Ground

> Establish clear figure-ground separation so users know what to focus on.

**Severity:** `high`

## Rule

Foreground content needs sufficient contrast against background. Decorative backgrounds must not compete with primary content.

## Do

- Maintain ≥4.5:1 contrast for body text
- Use surface tokens for layered depth
- Dim background imagery behind text

## Don't

- Place text directly over busy photographs without overlay
- Use low-contrast text for 'aesthetic' reasons
