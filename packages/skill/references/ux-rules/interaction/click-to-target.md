# Click Targets Match Affordance

> Visual affordance and click area must match exactly.

**Severity:** `high`

## Rule

If it looks clickable, it IS clickable across its full visual extent. No 'dead zones' inside buttons or cards.

## Do

- Make entire card clickable when card is link
- Pad icon buttons so click area = visible button

## Don't

- Clickable text inside non-clickable card
- Tiny click area inside large button
